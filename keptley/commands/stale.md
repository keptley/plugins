description: Pages that may be out of date, and what to do about each
---

Show the documentation that may no longer be true.

1. Call `keptley_doubts`.
2. For each page: what was changed, which pull request changed it, why Keptley thinks the page may
   now be wrong, and who should be asked.
3. Order them by what would mislead somebody soonest, not by date.
4. Offer to fix the top one. If I agree, read it with `keptley_page`, propose the edit, and only
   save with `keptley_save_doc` after I have approved the wording. Then call `keptley_resolve_doubt`.
