Show what sessions produced, so I do not write again what somebody already wrote.

1. Call `keptley_produced`. Read **$ARGUMENTS** as whichever it is: a kind (`artifact`, `doc`, `note`,

   last 30 days.
2. Group by repository, then by month, newest first. For each one give the title, what it is, who made
   it, and the session's own summary where there is one.
3. Say which of them nobody has opened yet. That is the most useful thing on the list and it is counted,
   not guessed.
4. Finish with the one or two closest to what I am working on now, and say plainly if none of them is.
   Do not summarise a document you have not read: give me the link and offer to read it.
